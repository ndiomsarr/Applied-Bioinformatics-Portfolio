# Applied Bioinformatics Portfolio

A progressive portfolio of practical bioinformatics projects covering sequencing, sequence analysis, genome annotation, programming, genomics, proteomics, and biological databases.

## About

This portfolio documents my learning journey and practical work in bioinformatics, from fundamental sequence analysis to more advanced computational approaches.

The projects are organized progressively to reflect my development of bioinformatics skills.

## Projects

### 01. Sanger Sequencing

Analysis of Sanger sequencing data, including chromatogram quality assessment, sequence trimming, assembly, and consensus sequence generation.

### 02. BLAST Sequence Identification

Identification and characterization of unknown DNA sequences using BLAST and interpretation of sequence similarity results.

### 03. Sequence Alignment

DNA and protein sequence alignment, identification of conserved regions, and analysis of sequence variation.

### 04. Genome Annotation

Annotation of genomic sequences and identification of genes, coding sequences, and functional information.

### 05. Genome Analysis

Exploration and comparative analysis of genomic features and biological information.

### 06. Synthetic Gene Design

In silico design and analysis of synthetic gene sequences.

### 07. Linux for Bioinformatics

Application of Linux command-line tools and Bash scripting to biological data analysis.

### 08. Python for Bioinformatics

Development of Python-based bioinformatics analyses using:

- Biopython
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib

### 09. R for Bioinformatics

Application of R for biological data processing, statistical analysis, and visualization, with an introduction to Bioconductor.

### 10. Genomics

Analysis of genomic data and introduction to computational approaches for genomics.

### 11. Proteomics

Analysis of protein sequences, properties, motifs, domains, and proteomics-related biological data.

### 12. Biological Databases

Exploration and comparison of biological databases to retrieve, analyze, and interpret molecular and genomic information.
